# Speeding up to something we're proud of

_Posted 5 September 2026 — a message to the Nanzen team._

Hey team, sharing here some reflections from this week.

I know that some of you, me included, have been craving more clarity and certainty in the short run. Having clear validation signals like customers lining up to use our product so we just need to worry about building it and scaling it (which is where we are probably most comfortable).

However, the 0 to 1 journey of a company is something extremely non-linear that requires work, persistence, humbleness, adaptability and working fast and hard. It tests your optimism and your faith. But that's the beauty of it. After we clear it, we will never be in this stage again and we'll probably miss it a bit, so let's embrace it.

**We have all the ingredients to cook something great**

We have been working a lot these last months on bringing clarity to our product proposition to hit Product-Market Fit. We have decided to build Nanzen as a true finance Ops agentic OS, focusing first on the buyer flow and not the seller. We have produced a number of documents that we shared with you that bring a lot of clarity.

- [Product Strategy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EvIutCmMtQvoOsL9TrBdm_5-QBEBeItFeK8JR6lxDBQ/edit?usp=sharing)
- [Product Requirements Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Yr9UuRt7YRN-vyAmMylzDQzt-Vb0Je8P_0R72n4QbU4/edit?usp=sharing)
- [Fintech Specification](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ch7NWb6WAv-Sh--9zQjIZkL3wZz-E4NUZk0VcMc5cH0/edit?usp=sharing)
- [Partner Map](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18dcU_jP8HVbfZF5OXqmjTUzarIa1gjYk58JNdDyVvIk/edit?usp=sharing)

The important bit is that we have another shot at showing Nanzen to prospects and we can't waste the opportunity. We need to show a product **we are proud of** and we need to tell a story that walks the user into the future. For us this means that Nanzen

- Should feel alive
- Solves a real problem for them, so customers can relate
- Is trustworthy: numbers are always accurate and actions make sense
- Is understandable: users can easily find out what's going on and what's expected of them

**What's the bottleneck?**

We won't get a prize for having the most organized repo. We need to always think _what's the bottleneck?_ and make sure that whichever action we decide to take next helps us reach our immediate next milestone.

For us the bottleneck is landing more design partners so we hit our plan of 1M ARR.

This means that some technical debt is expected and good. Remember: _Tech debt is the battle scar of the survivor_.

This does not mean that we delegate our thinking to AI and suddenly have zero governance of our code or product. We need to strike a balance.

**Nanzen is an energy source**

For me, being together in one room this week has been truly energizing. Seeing Arnau and Gon, having Luis and PT join, Andrei going the extra mile to join Nanzen makes me feel like the sky's the limit.

(Yes, the meeting room wasn't perfect but it's just temporary and next month we should have _the most beautiful office in Amsterdam_)

Being together is also more efficient. A 1h sync session between Fredy and me would have prevented Fredy from undoing some work (sorry Fredy, but thanks for that!).

But that's the important bit: Nanzen gives me joy and energy, it's not just a job.

PT asked me yesterday if it was OK to work on the weekend, and said he wouldn't send Slack messages so he didn't bother the others. My answer was: we work on weekends, not office hours. Nanzen is not a 9-to-5 job. We are building the future together, and that makes us use the spare hours, when the family doesn't need us, to log in, find things out, share things, read things, finish things instead of watching a movie. It's not a push, it's a pull.

And remember, not everything we ship will see the light of day. But work is compounding: every thing we learn, do, fix, ship, nuke or decide compounds and gets us closer to making Nanzen an era-defining company.

Enjoy the weekend
